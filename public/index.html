How it works

From search to magnet.

1. Choose your sources and a category, then search a title or browse the latest releases.

2. Results arrive as each source responds. Duplicate hashes are combined, preserving their trackers. Unavailable sources are shown in the sidebar.

3. Select “Get magnet” to inspect, copy, save or open the magnet in your own torrent client. You can also paste an existing magnet or a 40-character hex / 32-character base32 info hash.

Saved links stay in this browser. Providers are queried through this site's server. Some sources may be unavailable or return no matching releases. Only download content you have permission to access.
